Privacy Policy

Your workflow stays yours.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Orvesca is a Windows desktop workflow tool. This policy explains what information Orvesca handles, where it is kept, and who it is shared with. Orvesca does not require a user account, and your workflow data stays on your own computer.

Who we are

"Orvesca", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the makers of the Orvesca application and website (orvesca.github.io). You can reach us any time at orvescaapp@gmail.com.

Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to both the Orvesca website and the Orvesca desktop application. Because the website and the application serve different purposes, they collect and process different types of information. The sections below explain how information is handled in each case.

Website

Website analytics

The Orvesca website uses Google Analytics to help us understand website traffic, improve content, and enhance the overall user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate geographic region, browser and device information, operating system, referring websites, and general usage statistics. This information is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy.

The website analytics described in this section apply only to visits to the Orvesca website. They are separate from the Orvesca desktop application and are not used to monitor application usage.

Information handled when you buy a plan

Purchases are processed by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record for every sale. When you buy the Annual Pass or Lifetime Pro, you enter your email and payment details on Lemon Squeezy's secure checkout — not inside Orvesca. Lemon Squeezy collects and processes that purchase and payment information under its own privacy policy.

We do not run our own servers that store your name, email, or payment details. We receive only what Lemon Squeezy provides to fulfil your order (such as your order details and license key).

Information stored on your device

Orvesca stores a small amount of data locally on your own computer, in files that never leave your device except for the license check described below:

License file

To manage your free trial and keep your paid plan active, Orvesca saves a local license file. For everyone, it holds the date your trial started and the most recent time the app was used (so the trial is counted correctly). For paid plans, it also holds your license key, license type, subscription status, the customer name associated with the purchase, a device identifier generated for your installation, and timestamps for activation and the most recent online check. The file is stored in a hidden .orvesca folder in your Windows user folder (%USERPROFILE%\.orvesca) and carries a signature that lets Orvesca detect manual changes.

Workflow file

The workflow you build — the order of your windows, your chosen jump keys, whether jumping is on, your notes, and whether each note is shown — is saved in local files in the app's own data folder on your computer. This workflow data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Windows Orvesca can see

To let you build a workflow, Orvesca reads the list of open windows shown in your Windows taskbar (for example, their titles) so you can add them to your path, reorder them, and jump between them. Orvesca does not read, capture, record, or transmit the contents of those windows — only their presence in the taskbar, and only on your device in real time. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

License verification

For paid plans, Orvesca contacts Lemon Squeezy's licensing service once a week to confirm your license is still active and to allow use across the devices you manage. If the device is offline, the check is retried and your plan keeps working for a grace period of up to three weeks in total; you can also trigger a check yourself with the Sync button.

Each check sends your license key and your installation's device identifier, and receives back your activation status and the customer name shown in the app. No workflow data, notes, window titles, or window contents are included in this check.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use tracking pixels.
  • We do not build user profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not require you to create an account to use Orvesca.
  • We do not send your workflows, notes, window titles, window contents, or other personal work data to our servers or use them for analytics.

Retention and control

Because the license and workflow files live on your computer, you remain in control of them. Uninstalling Orvesca removes your workflow data and settings. The license file is intentionally kept after uninstalling, so that reinstalling Orvesca does not lose your activation or reset your trial; you can remove it at any time by deleting the %USERPROFILE%\.orvesca folder. For purchase-related information held by Lemon Squeezy, you can exercise your rights through Lemon Squeezy's customer portal or by contacting us and we will help direct your request.

Third-party services

The Orvesca website and desktop application use different third-party services for specific purposes:

  • Google Analytics is used only on the Orvesca website to understand website traffic and improve the site. It is not used by the desktop application.
  • Lemon Squeezy processes purchases, manages software licenses, and performs license verification for the desktop application. Information related to purchases and licensing is processed according to Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Orvesca website. The application is distributed through the Microsoft Store and GitHub Releases, which are used only to deliver downloads and updates.

Each service is used only for its specific purpose as described above.

Children

Orvesca is a productivity tool intended for a general, working audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Orvesca evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Orvesca after an update means you accept the revised policy.
